# Design direction

The website uses a calm editorial visual language: warm ivory surfaces, deep forest green text and panels, and restrained copper accents. Newsreader carries the display voice; DM Sans carries navigation and body copy. Spacious typography and fine dividers structure the page. A subtle orbit motif gives movement a visual presence without implying a clinical claim or replacing real photography.

The opening screen pairs a large typographic introduction with an intentionally empty portrait frame. Services read as a list, not a grid of generic cards. A deep green approach section creates the primary change in rhythm. Motion is limited to a composed opening and gentle section reveals, with a reduced motion fallback.

The reference composition is `.impeccable/mocks/composition.png`. The implemented page follows its visual direction while expanding the full content flow and avoiding the generated mockup's unverified claims.
